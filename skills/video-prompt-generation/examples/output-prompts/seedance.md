# 示例输出：Seedance

## 优化后提示词

Open with a clean, fast hook: a stylish woman steps into a dusk street frame and the movement of her long coat is immediately visible. Cut quickly to her adjusting the collar, then a low-angle boot step detail, then a side-follow shot as she passes a shop window. End with her stopping and turning back to camera in a confident final pose. Keep the pacing sharp, the outfit details stable, and the city background controlled so the viewer reads the fashion focus instantly.

## 提示词拆解

- 钩子：开头立刻出现风衣轮廓和人物动作。
- 中段证明：领口动作、靴子特写、橱窗经过，持续强化穿搭重点。
- 适配重点：让每个镜头目的单一清楚，适合短时长快节奏内容。

## 建议避免项

- 不要把看点放得太晚
- 不要背景过乱抢走穿搭主体
- 不要让服装轮廓在镜头间变化
