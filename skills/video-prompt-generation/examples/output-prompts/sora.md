# 示例输出：Sora

## 优化后提示词

In a quiet sunlit kitchen, a young woman enters the space and places a mug beside the coffee machine. She presses the button, coffee begins to flow, and light steam rises into the morning light. She lifts the mug, walks to the window, pauses to smell the coffee, then takes a small sip while the room remains calm and consistent. Keep the environment stable, the motion natural, and the mood soft and grounded.

## 提示词拆解

- 连续动作：进入厨房、操作机器、端杯移动、窗边停留，像一件事自然发生。
- 场景稳定：厨房、窗边、杯子和咖啡机的关系保持不变。
- 适配重点：强调连续世界状态，不把内容拆成互相断开的镜头标签。

## 建议避免项

- 不要突然切换成另一个厨房
- 不要让人物动作断裂
- 不要出现不合理的蒸汽或杯子形变
