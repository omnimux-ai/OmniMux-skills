# Recreate Reference Video Template

## Use Case
Use this template when the user wants to:
- recreate a reference video for their own product
- adapt a competitor ad into an original concept
- reuse a winning video structure without copying it directly
- preserve the commercial logic of a reference while changing the product, tone, or execution
- generate multiple original concept directions based on one source video

This template is for strategic adaptation, not literal imitation.

## Template Rules
1. Do not recreate reference videos mechanically.
2. Always identify what makes the reference commercially effective first.
3. Always test whether the user's product can honestly support the same logic.
4. Separate transferable selling structure from non-transferable expression.
5. Make variants meaningfully different, not just reworded.
6. Protect product truth over surface similarity.
7. If the fit is weak, redesign the structure instead of forcing imitation.
8. Keep the adaptation useful for scripting, storyboarding, or prompt generation.
9. The final concept should stand on its own, even if inspired by the reference.
10. Strategic inspiration is the goal; direct copying is not.
