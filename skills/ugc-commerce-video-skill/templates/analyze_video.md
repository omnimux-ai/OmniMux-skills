# Analyze Video Template

## Use Case
Use this template when the user wants to:
- analyze a short-form ecommerce video
- break down a TikTok Shop or UGC selling video
- understand why a video works or underperforms
- study a competitor or winning creative
- extract reusable content lessons from a reference video

This template is designed for commercial analysis, not generic video commentary.

## Template Rules
1. Do not only describe what happens in the video.
2. Always explain what each section is doing commercially.
3. Always evaluate the first 1–3 seconds carefully.
4. Always separate claims from proof.
5. Always assess whether the product is understandable early enough.
6. Always identify likely viewer skepticism.
7. Always judge the CTA in context, not in isolation.
8. Prioritize a few high-impact recommendations over generic advice.
9. If no performance data exists, frame conclusions as reasoned analysis rather than certainty.
10. Keep the report specific, practical, and useful for the next creative decision.
