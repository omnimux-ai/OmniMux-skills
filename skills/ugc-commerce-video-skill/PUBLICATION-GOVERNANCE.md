# UGC Commerce Video Publication Governance

## Decision

`video/ugc-commerce-video-skill` remains a public business skill because its primary value is short-form commerce video analysis, scripts, storyboards, prompt packs, reference adaptation, hook/CTA variants, and creator briefs.

It is not treated as a code-execution skill.

## Runtime Boundary

Runtime-readable entrypoint:

- `SKILL.md`

Supporting folders such as `prompts/`, `schemas/`, `templates/`, `examples/`, and `docs/` provide method detail and calibration. They are not hidden workflow owners.

## Public Use Conditions

- Use for ecommerce UGC analysis, conversion scripting, storyboard planning, prompt conversion, safe reference adaptation, variants, and creator briefs.
- Do not generate final videos unless the user explicitly requests generation and a visible video tool returns evidence.
- Do not invent product claims, certifications, rankings, testimonials, or sales data.
- Do not copy a reference video exactly; adapt structure and persuasion mechanics safely.
