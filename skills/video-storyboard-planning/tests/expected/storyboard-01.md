# Portable Blender TikTok Shop UGC

## Project
- **Platform:** TikTok
- **Format:** 9:16
- **Duration:** 24s
- **Language:** zh-CN
- **Video Type:** ecommerce_conversion

## Summary
- **Objective:** Sell a portable blender through a relatable busy-morning problem and quick demo sequence.
- **Target Audience:** Busy women aged 22-35 who want faster wellness routines.
- **Core Selling Points:** portable cup design, one-button blending, easy morning routine
- **CTA:** Tap to view the product
- **Creative Strategy:** Open with a real morning pain point, show one clear product demo, then close with a natural creator CTA.

## Structure

| Section | Time | Goal | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0s-3s | Stop scroll with a familiar rushed-morning problem. | Creator shows breakfast chaos and says she has no time. |
| Demo | 3s-18s | Show the product as a simple solution. | Product reveal, fruit loading, one-button blending, and portability moment. |
| CTA | 18s-24s | Convert interest into a product click. | Creator drinks, shows the product clearly, and gives a natural CTA. |

## Prompt Hints

- **01:** Bright kitchen UGC shot, rushed creator, banana, gym bag, vertical framing.
- **02:** White portable blender close-up, fruit and milk added, clean kitchen counter.
- **03:** Portable blender cup with travel lid, gym bag side pocket, fast UGC detail shot.
- **04:** Selfie UGC outside gym, creator holding white portable blender clearly.

## Rhythm Notes

- Hook must land in the first 2 seconds.
- Demo shots should use fast, readable cuts.
- CTA should feel like creator recommendation, not a hard-sell ad.

## Production Notes

- Maintain the same white blender shape across all shots.
- Use vertical 9:16 framing with space for subtitles.
- Avoid medical, detox, or weight-loss claims.

## Compliance Notes

- Do not imply unsupported ice-crushing performance.
- Avoid health outcome guarantees.
