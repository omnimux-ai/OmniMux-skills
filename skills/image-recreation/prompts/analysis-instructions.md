# Reference Image Analysis

Use this reference when the task needs a structured breakdown of one or more reference images before planning recreation.

## Analysis Layers

### Visual Structure

- Composition: centered, diagonal, flat lay, split-screen, grid, hero-product, before/after, close-up, lifestyle scene.
- Subject hierarchy: what is the first focal point, second focal point, and supporting detail.
- Camera: angle, crop, lens feel, distance, depth of field, motion or stillness.
- Lighting: hard/soft, natural/studio, direction, shadow role, gloss or matte emphasis.
- Color: dominant palette, contrast, saturation, warmth, brand compatibility.
- Scene elements: props, background, surface, model/hand relation, text space.
- Material expression: texture, reflection, transparency, fabric, skin, packaging, liquid, metal, paper.

### Commercial Logic

- What is the image selling: product identity, feature proof, lifestyle desire, before/after result, trust, premium texture, convenience.
- How the selling point becomes visible: product scale, hand interaction, close-up detail, usage context, annotation space, contrast.
- What causes the viewer to stop: unusual angle, high contrast, clear benefit, emotional scene, product clarity, recognizable category cue.
- What must stay legible on mobile: product silhouette, core benefit, one key detail, brand or category signal.

### Platform Fit

- Main product image: clean background, clear product, low noise, minimal copy.
- Listing secondary image: functional proof, feature explanation, lifestyle scenario, comparison.
- Ad creative: stronger hook, emotion, contrast, bolder framing, campaign space.
- Social content: curiosity, atmosphere, human context, sharable visual rhythm.
- Detail/A+ content: modular information, feature callouts, text-safe areas.
