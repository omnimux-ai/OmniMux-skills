# 示例输出：Veo

## 优化后提示词

Create a polished lifestyle coffee video set in a clean kitchen at early morning, with soft sunlight shaping the counter, the mug, and the woman’s hands. Begin with a calm environmental view, move closer as she presses the coffee machine, then show a rich close-up of coffee pouring into the mug. Follow her as she carries the cup to the window, inhales the aroma, and takes a small sip. Keep the camera readable, the textures clean, and the commercial mood warm but believable.

## 提示词拆解

- 镜头重点：环境建立、咖啡流动特写、窗边收尾。
- 质感处理：强调光线、材质和可读构图，不只写情绪词。
- 适配重点：保护商业短片的完成度和产品可见性。

## 建议避免项

- 不要让光线忽冷忽热
- 不要让杯子或咖啡机外观漂移
- 不要让镜头运动过猛破坏生活感
