# 示例输出：Generic

## 优化后提示词

Create a warm morning coffee scene in a clean sunlit kitchen. A young woman places a mug beside the coffee machine, presses the button, and watches coffee pour into the cup. She lifts the mug, walks to the window, smells the coffee, and takes a relaxed sip. Keep the action order continuous, the lighting soft, and the atmosphere natural with light commercial polish.

## 提示词拆解

- 主体与场景：清晨厨房、女生、咖啡机、窗边收尾。
- 动作顺序：放杯子、按按钮、咖啡流动、走向窗边、喝一口。
- 风格处理：先保证生活化动作成立，再补柔和晨光和轻广告感。

## 建议避免项

- 不要背景杂乱
- 不要手部异常
- 不要让厨房空间在镜头间跳变
