# Build Storyboard Template

## Use Case
Use this template when the user wants to:
- turn a product idea into a shot-by-shot short video plan
- convert a script into a storyboard
- build a visual structure for a TikTok-style ecommerce video
- prepare a creator brief or filming guide
- create a scene plan before AI video generation
- make a short-form concept easier to film, edit, or delegate

This template is designed for commercial short-form execution, not general film storyboarding.

## Template Rules
1. Every scene must have a commercial purpose.
2. Do not create filler shots.
3. Show the product early enough for short-form viewing.
4. Make major claims visually provable.
5. Use overlays to clarify, not to carry the entire concept.
6. Keep the storyboard practical for filming or generation.
7. Do not sacrifice clarity for style.
8. Make sure the ending visually supports the CTA.
9. If the product needs proof more than mood, give proof more screen time.
10. The storyboard should feel like a visual persuasion map, not just a list of shots.
